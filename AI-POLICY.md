# AI usage policy

This policy states my preferences for AI use of my GitHub profile, content I own, and repositories where I publish or explicitly adopt this policy. It does not claim ownership of third-party content.

## Default restriction

The following uses are permitted without prior individual approval:

- Academic use, including teaching, research, and study.
- Using AI to read, analyze, explain, or understand my work.
- Allowing language models to learn from my code, including through dataset creation, training, fine-tuning, distillation, and evaluation.
- Using AI to assist with contributions, subject to the human responsibility requirements below.

These permissions include the access, copying, indexing, and processing needed for those purposes, including providing my code to AI services.

Automated scraping, collection, or profiling unrelated to these permitted purposes requires my explicit permission. Autonomous contributions and submissions without a responsible human who understands the work are not permitted.

## Human responsibility for contributions

Every contribution, including code, documentation, issues, pull requests, reviews, and security reports, must have a human contributor who takes responsibility for it. When using AI, that person must:

- Review and understand the complete contribution, including why each change is needed and how it affects the project.
- Verify its correctness using appropriate tests or checks.
- Be able to explain the work, answer review questions, and address problems with it.

The human contributor must review and approve the final contribution before submission. Submitting AI output without understanding it, or merely approving it without meaningful review, does not meet this requirement.

## Explicit authorization

Only activities outside the standing permissions above require separate authorization from me. Any additional permission applies only to the content, actions, and purpose I authorize.

A third party cannot grant additional permission on my behalf. If an activity falls outside the permitted uses and authorization is absent or unclear, stop rather than assume it.

Agents may read this policy and `./AGENTS.md` to determine which activities are permitted.

## Ordinary use

This policy does not restrict human browsing or conventional non-AI tools used for builds, tests, formatting, linting, or continuous integration under the project's existing rules.

## Licenses and access

This document expresses my AI-use preferences and contribution requirements. It does not amend an existing software license, revoke permissions granted under that license, or override GitHub's terms or applicable law. It is not a technical access control.
