# Agent instructions

The owner of this repository is `aalsanie`. Read [AI-POLICY.md](AI-POLICY.md) before proceeding.

Academic use, study, AI explanations of the work, and language-model learning from the code are permitted without individual owner approval. AI assistance with contributions is also permitted under these rules:

- Work with a human contributor who understands the complete contribution and takes responsibility for it.
- Prepare changes and explanations for human review. The human must verify, understand, and approve the final contribution before submission.
- Do not independently submit contributions or treat an unreviewed AI result as ready to submit.
- The human contributor must be able to explain the work and address review feedback or problems.

Do not scrape, collect, or profile content for purposes outside the permissions in the policy without explicit owner authorization. If a task falls outside those permissions, stop and explain the limitation to the requester. Do not contact the owner or open an issue automatically.
